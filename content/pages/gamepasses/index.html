Gamepass FAQ

Before you spend Robux.

Honest answers about value and timing.

Do Track or Police Pass include the vehicles?

No. The official disclaimers say vehicles must still be bought with in-game currency after purchasing either pass. The pass unlocks access; the dealership still charges cash.

Which pass is the best value?

For pure income, VIP's 50% boost is the only pass that pays you back over time — but the free 25% group boost covers casual play. Track and Police are playstyle unlocks, and Mobile Customization only matters on touch devices.

Can pass prices change?

Yes. Prices and sale status are mutable catalog data, rechecked here on August 20, 2026. Confirm the live price in-game before buying.

Is anything free instead?

The Midnight In-dev group join is free and advertises a 25% cash boost, playtime rewards hand out cash, and the current code is free. Passes accelerate; they never gate the basics.
