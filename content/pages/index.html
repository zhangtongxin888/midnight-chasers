Common mistakes

Four ways new drivers stall.

Most slow starts come from the same handful of habits. Avoid these and the cash keeps flowing.

✕

Skipping the free group boost

Joining the Midnight In-dev group is free and advertises a 25% cash boost. Driving without it is leaving money on the table.

✕

Expecting passes to gift cars

Track Pass and Police Pass do not include the vehicles — official disclaimers say you still buy them with in-game cash.

✕

Mistyping the code

Codes are reported as case-sensitive, and pasted leading or trailing spaces make them fail. Enter ThanksFor940k exactly.

✕

Trusting old code lists

Milestone codes expire, and third-party tables already disagree with the official description. The game page is the only current list.
