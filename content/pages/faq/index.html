Gameplay

Playing the highway.

Deeper answers live on the linked system pages.

What is Midnight Chasers about?

A no-hesi highway cut-up game: swerve through traffic in supercars, hypercars, or regular cars — or take a regular drive. Racing, exploring with friends, and meetups are official features too.

How do I earn cash?

Primarily by driving. Races, playtime rewards, showcase events, and occasional milestone codes add more — and the free 25% group boost multiplies it. See the boosts page.

Is racing required?

No. The Win a Race badge exists, but its award rate is far below the Welcome badge — cruising, farming, and meetups are equally official ways to play.

What badges exist?

Welcome! (join the game), Win a Race (first race win), Richie (acquire 1,000,000 Money), and The Hatch — a historical event badge no longer being awarded.

How many vehicles are there?

Official copy promises a large choice of cars and motorcycles. A September 2025 guide counted 155, but that number is dated — see the vehicles page for honest context.

Codes & boosts

Free cash questions.

The short version — with links to the full pages.

What is the current code?

ThanksFor940k, printed in the official game description and checked August 20, 2026. The next code unlocks at 960000 likes. Redeem steps on the codes page.

How do I redeem a code?

Open the Store control (yellow icon on the left HUD under Spawn, or the tickets button some guides show bottom-left), choose the Codes tab, enter the code exactly — case-sensitive, no stray spaces — and press Redeem.

Do the group and VIP boosts stack?

Officially unanswered. The game page advertises 25% (group) and 50% (VIP) separately and never states a combined figure. Details on the boosts page.

Do Track or Police Pass give free cars?

No. Official disclaimers say vehicles still cost in-game cash after either pass. Full catalog on the gamepasses page.

Community & lookalikes

Know the real game.

The mix-ups that send drivers to the wrong highway.

Is this Midnight Racing: Tokyo?

No. This is Midnight Chasers: Highway Racing by Midnight In-dev (place 13822562292). Tokyo is a map referenced inside this game — not the game's title, and not a different game called Midnight Racing: Tokyo.

Is there a Discord or X account?

Guides cite the community Discord (discord.com/invite/bQWFVXVqRj), and @MidnightIndev on X identifies itself as the official Twitter page for the game.

Is the Fandom wiki official?

No. Treat fan wikis as unofficial community notes, and do not confuse them with this site — neither is run by the developers. The official game page is the authority.

Is this site official?

No. Midnight Chasers Guide is an independent player resource, not affiliated with Midnight In-dev or Roblox. Facts carry a check date, and the in-game screen always wins.
