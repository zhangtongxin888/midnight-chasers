Boost FAQ

Boost questions, answered.

The stacking question and everything around it.

Do group and VIP boosts stack?

Nobody official says. The two boosts are advertised separately, and no combined figure is published — treat any quoted total as unverified and test in-game if it matters to you.

Is the group boost really free?

Yes. Joining the Midnight In-dev Roblox group costs nothing, and the game page advertises a 25% cash boost for members. There is no reason to skip it.

Is VIP worth 200 Robux?

Only if you drive regularly: the 50% boost pays off over long sessions, and VIP adds a tag plus extra customization build slots. Casual players do fine on the free group boost alone. Prices checked August 20, 2026.

Does Roblox Premium give a cash boost?

No boost is advertised — Premium members get an in-game tag, per the official page.
